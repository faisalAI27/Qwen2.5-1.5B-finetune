# Publication assessment

Reviewed 2026-11-17 after the complete results. The delivered eight-page project report is an attributed research draft with appendices, not a venue-formatted or submitted manuscript.

A provisional publication target is an ACL-family short (non-)reproduction/negative-result paper through ACL Rolling Review (ARR). ARR is a reviewing service; a final conference/workshop commitment is a separate decision. This scoped outcome fits the types described in the [official ARR call](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp). No acceptance, eligibility or final venue commitment is implied.

The checked call permits four content pages for short papers, with required Limitations after the conclusion and separate reference space. It requires two-way anonymization and contains publication ethics and responsible NLP requirements. Starting October 2026, guaranteed review is tied to a qualified service contributor; submissions without contributed service can be subject to a lottery. Accurate OpenReview profiles are required. These conditions require the authors' real profiles and contributions; they cannot be supplied by this project code. See the [call](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp) and [current dates/venue schedule](https://aclrollingreview.org/dates), checked on the review date. No future deadline is assumed or represented as booked.

Before choosing to submit, the authors must select an actual accepting venue/cycle, recheck its current call/deadline, compress the main narrative to its official template/page limit, prepare an anonymized version, complete the responsible NLP checklist and AI-assistance disclosure, resolve author/profile/service eligibility, and review redistribution terms. These are optional external publication steps; the completed project handoff does not send or submit anything. A license grant is not selected on the authors' behalf. No new GPU run is required to read or assess the existing evidence, although stronger causal research claims would need further experiments.
